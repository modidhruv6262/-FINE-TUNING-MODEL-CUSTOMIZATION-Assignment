Tournament: Indian Premier League (IPL) Historical & Season Statistics

Record 1: Most Career Runs in IPL - Virat Kohli leads the all-time scoring charts with 8004 runs in 252 matches at an average of 38.66 and a strike rate of 131.97, including 8 centuries and 55 half-centuries.
Record 2: Highest Individual Score - Chris Gayle holds the record for the highest individual score in IPL history, smashing 175 not out off 66 balls for Royal Challengers Bangalore against Pune Warriors India in 2013.
Record 3: Highest Team Total - Sunrisers Hyderabad scored the highest team total in IPL history, posting 287/3 in 20 overs against Royal Challengers Bengaluru at M. Chinnaswamy Stadium on April 15, 2024.
Record 4: Most Career Wickets in IPL - Yuzvendra Chahal is the highest wicket-taker in IPL history with 205 wickets in 160 matches with an economy rate of 7.84 and best bowling figures of 5/40.
Record 5: Most IPL Championships Won - Chennai Super Kings and Mumbai Indians share the joint record for most tournament titles, having won 5 IPL trophies each (CSK: 2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023; MI: 2013, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2020).
Record 6: Fastest Half-Century in IPL - Yashasvi Jaiswal scored the fastest fifty in IPL history off just 13 balls for Rajasthan Royals against Kolkata Knight Riders at Eden Gardens in 2023.
Record 7: Best Bowling Figures in a Match - Alzarri Joseph recorded 6/12 in 3.4 overs for Mumbai Indians against Sunrisers Hyderabad in 2019.
Record 8: IPL 2024 Orange Cap Winner - Virat Kohli won the Orange Cap in IPL 2024 scoring 741 runs in 15 innings at an average of 61.75 and strike rate of 154.69.
Record 9: IPL 2024 Purple Cap Winner - Harshal Patel won the Purple Cap in IPL 2024 taking 24 wickets in 14 matches for Punjab Kings.
Record 10: IPL 2024 Champions - Kolkata Knight Riders won the IPL 2024 championship, defeating Sunrisers Hyderabad by 8 wickets in the final at MA Chidambaram Stadium, Chennai.
